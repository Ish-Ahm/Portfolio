Game Jam - Develish Monkey

The game was developed as part of a game jam, where I collaborated with a team to create a fun and engaging experience within a tight timeframe. Tools like Aseprite (for pixel art) and Unity (for game development) were used to bring the concept to life. The game features a unique mechanic where players control a mischievous monkey navigating through various levels, solving puzzles, and avoiding obstacles.

The project involved rapid prototyping, iterative design, and playtesting to refine the gameplay and ensure a smooth user experience. The art style was chosen to be vibrant and appealing, enhancing the overall aesthetic of the game. Sound effects and music were also incorporated to create an immersive environment for players.
